# ADR-NNN: Short title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN
- **PRD reference:** section and decision ID
- **Deciders:** who decided; who drafted

## Context

What problem or constraint forces a decision? Two to four sentences.

## Decision

- What we will do, as short bullet points.

## Consequences

- What gets easier, what gets harder, what must be maintained.

## Alternatives considered

- Each option and why it was not chosen.

## Verification

- How we will know the decision is implemented and still holds (tests, gates, reports).

## Revisit when

- Concrete triggers that reopen the decision.

Rules: one decision per ADR; never edit an accepted ADR in place, supersede it with a
new one; numbers 001-014 map to PRD decisions D1-D14, later ADRs continue from 015.
